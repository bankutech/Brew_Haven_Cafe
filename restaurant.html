House Guidelines

Dress Code

We request elegant smart-casual attire. Gentlemen are required to wear collared shirts. Jackets are preferred. Athletic wear and open-toed shoes for gentlemen are strictly prohibited.

Dining Etiquette

To preserve the intimacy of our dining room, we kindly ask that phone conversations be taken to the lobby. Flash photography is not permitted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?
Yes, please inform us of any severe allergies at least 48 hours prior to your reservation. Our chef will craft bespoke alternatives.
Is there a corkage fee?
We allow a maximum of two 750ml bottles per table at ₹7055 per bottle.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations must be made 72 hours in advance to avoid a ₹8300 per person fee.
Is valet parking available?
Yes, complimentary valet parking is available for all guests dining from 6 PM onwards.
